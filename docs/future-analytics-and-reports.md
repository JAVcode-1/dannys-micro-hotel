# Future Analytics & Reports


Owner-revised scope recorded 2026-11-20. This supersedes the earlier six-feature proposal. This specifies future StayTrack software, not an existing hotel reporting process or an implemented dashboard. Implement after remaining approved core features and before Function Hall Reservation & Management. See [[13-development-roadmap]].

## Only three approved features

| Feature | Required reporting |
|---|---|
| Revenue Analytics | Daily, weekly and monthly revenue trends from trustworthy transaction and financial records, without double counting. |
| Room Category Performance | Revenue generated by each approved accommodation category; derive categories dynamically to support future additions. Keep future Function Hall revenue separately identifiable. |
| Package Popularity | Most frequently selected duration packages, booking frequency and revenue contribution per package. Respect category-specific package availability and historical package snapshots; no Family Executive 3-hour package. |

All three require date-range filtering, CSV/Excel report exports and staff authorization enforced through Express. Retain Asia/Manila reporting with user-facing times in 12-hour AM/PM format. Exports use the same authorization, filters and calculation definitions as the dashboard, exclude private proofs and unnecessary guest/payer data, and neutralize spreadsheet formula injection in exported text.

## Calculation safeguards

- Use authoritative booking and immutable financial records. Never multiply reservation-level money by joining it to multiple room items, packages or payment allocations.
- Count factual financial events once. Partial payments, receipt allocations, extensions, credits, waivers and completed refunds must not duplicate revenue or package contribution. Unverified proof and pending extension commitments are not verified receipts or earned revenue.
- Distinguish actual collections from earned revenue internally when deriving revenue trends; do not label deposits as earned revenue. Recognition timing and allocation across service periods, categories and packages require reviewed definitions before implementation acceptance; no accounting policy is invented here.
- Reconcile category/package contributions with the same authoritative revenue basis, preserving independent multi-room cancellation/rescheduling/extension history. Distinguish original package selections from later extensions so package frequency and contribution cannot silently double count.
- Convert Asia/Manila reporting date boundaries to UTC consistently. Use historical booked prices rather than recomputing from current catalog rates.

These are correctness requirements for the three approved features, not additional payment, balance or booking dashboards.

## Explicit scope exclusions and operational boundary

Payment & Outstanding Balance Reports, Room Occupancy Analytics, Booking Analytics and Peak Hours & Demand Analytics are excluded as dedicated analytics/dashboard features. Do not reintroduce them as alternate metrics, cards, dashboards or exports. AI forecasting and complex customer profiling remain excluded.

Existing operational room availability, check-in/out, payments, balances, manual refunds, booking management and stay-duration monitoring remain in core scope. This revision removes no operational workflow or existing acceptance requirement.

## Future Function Hall compatibility

Keep future hall revenue separately identifiable from accommodation-category revenue without requiring a complete reporting redesign. All previously confirmed hall requirements, including its later separate revenue, booking and utilization reporting, remain in [[future-function-hall-requirements]] for the later Function Hall module. They do not expand this three-feature Analytics implementation. No hall implementation, schema or dependency is selected here.

## Future acceptance evidence (not executed)

Require fixtures for trustworthy daily/weekly/monthly revenue totals, partial/mixed payments, retries, extensions, partial room-item cancellations, credits and refunds without double counting. Demonstrate dynamic category revenue, category-specific package frequency/contribution, historical pricing, separate future hall revenue, local date boundaries, staff/guest authorization, and CSV/Excel parity and export safety. All implementation and acceptance remain pending; existing project acceptance gaps remain open and live transactions remain disabled.
