# H04 Verified Reviews and Private Feedback

This specification records the approved H04 development decisions following checkpoint `20a1124`. It implements the feature described in [Verified Guest Reviews](../../24-verified-guest-reviews.md). Public activation requires hotel-owner approval of the final moderation, privacy and retention policies and completion of the H04 acceptance gate.

## Eligibility and identity

The verified primary guest qualifies when any room item on the reservation has both a recorded staff check-in and a subsequent completed checkout for that same item. The first qualifying checkout opens a 30-day public submission window. Use database time and reject submissions at or after the expiry instant. Incomplete or cancelled siblings do not invalidate qualifying evidence. Enforce one public rating per parent reservation across all channels and states, including withdrawal.

Registered guests use existing Supabase Auth verification in Express. Match the verified identity ID and email to the stored reservation owner. Online guests without an account use a reservation-bound invitation delivered to the original verified booking email. For walk-ins, bind an optional email to the primary guest and reservation through staff-assisted enrollment and proof of email control using the existing OTP mechanism. Establish the reservation binding through the staff-assisted flow rather than a self-claimed reservation identifier. Keep enrollment and invitation consent optional for accommodation. Delivery requires consent and a verified binding.

Issue 24-hour single-use invitations within the public submission window. The submission deadline still applies when the invitation expires later. Rate-limit replacements and revoke superseded unused tokens atomically. Store a token digest, expiry, redemption and revocation timestamps, and delivery attempts. Use an opaque token, generic issue and redemption responses where ownership is unknown, and a secure delivery boundary that keeps secrets out of logs, URLs sent to the API, and public payloads. Redeem with an atomic consume operation into short-lived reservation-scoped access. Public rating and private feedback are independent permissions. Existing ratings neither disable private feedback nor permit a second rating.

The approved 30-day limit applies to public review submission. Apply verified completed-stay access to private feedback and withdrawal without deriving a separate policy deadline. Use fresh verified access when an invitation-derived claim expires. For verified walk-in or no-account access after the invitation issuance window, reuse the stored contact binding and existing email OTP to authenticate a reservation-scoped feedback or withdrawal action. The public submission deadline remains enforced.

## Records and transactional writes

Use private PostgreSQL tables for public review records, invitation and contact bindings, private feedback, staff replies, and append-only action history. Revoke direct browser writes and private reads. Give public results an explicit Express response projection. Use a unique reservation key for public reviews, integer stars from 1 to 5, an optional bounded plain-text comment, an automatically generated alias with no identity-derived content, and recorded public-submission consent. Keep stars and comment immutable. Treat withdrawal as a separately authorized and audited owner action that removes public visibility while retaining the one-rating constraint.

Recheck eligibility, verified identity, consent and relevant deadline inside the write transaction. Bind request keys to the actor or verified claim and payload. An exact authorized retry returns the recorded outcome. A changed payload with the same key or a competing second rating returns a conflict. Apply the same retry discipline to invitation redemption and moderation actions, with replay protection for consumed invitation credentials. Store immutable action history with actor, database timestamp, subject and prior/new state where applicable.

## Publication, moderation and private feedback

Publish compliant eligible ratings of every star value. Public list, count, distribution and average use only published reviews. Public fields include the generated alias, rating, optional comment, publication date and separate public staff reply. Omit reservation IDs and references, contact details, staff identity identifiers, private feedback and internal moderation evidence. Escape plain text in all views and prevent public replies from exposing booking details.

Use active-staff authorization to hold, hide or restore a review. Require an approved reason category and a documented explanation, and record the transition atomically. Objective reasons include spam or fraud, threats, harassment or discriminatory abuse, sensitive disclosure, and unrelated or prohibited content. Criticism, complaints and low ratings are ineligible reasons. Display the approved hotel contact channel for verified guests seeking reconsideration after owner signoff. Keep staff replies separate and attributed in private audit history.

Provide an active-staff private inbox with New, In Progress and Resolved statuses and an audited transition history. Collect follow-up consent before staff may use verified contact information. Private feedback has its own submission and response projection. Keep retention and privacy-request handling subject to owner policy and omit automatic permanent deletion. Keep public consent and private follow-up consent distinct.

## Integration and acceptance

Add Express route groups for public reviews and aggregates, invitation redemption, verified review and feedback actions, and staff moderation and feedback. Reuse existing guest and active-staff verifiers, transaction conventions, and staff shell. Route React views through `/reviews`, `/reviews/submit`, `/feedback/private`, `/staff/reviews` and `/staff/feedback`. Keep all H04 gates closed while developing with a disposable PostgreSQL database and simulated invitation delivery. Candidate migrations stay unapplied to hosted Supabase. Keep existing production mutation gates, hosted reconciliation blockers and unrelated features outside this change.

Run T31–T38 and focused coverage for the exact 30-day and 24-hour boundaries, original-email ownership, OTP contact binding, superseded invitations, consent, replay and parallel submissions, multi-room sibling isolation, withdrawal, repeated moderation requests, genuine one-star publication, aggregate accuracy and private inbox authorization. Test that public projections and errors omit sensitive fields and that disabled routes cannot send invitations or accept writes. Verify build, typecheck, lint, formatting, full application tests and isolated PostgreSQL tests. Obtain one final independent review during implementation. Activation additionally requires the owner-approved policies, approved contact channel and moderation pilot acceptance.
